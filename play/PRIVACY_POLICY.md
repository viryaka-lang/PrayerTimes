# Privacy Policy — Rabat Prayer Times

_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

Rabat Prayer Times shows prayer times for Rabat, Morocco, from a table built into the app.

**Data we collect: none.**

- The app uses the internet for one thing only: about twice a month, when a new Hijri month
  is announced, it downloads the public table of Moroccan Hijri month starts from GitHub
  (github.com/viryaka-lang/HijriCalendarMaghrib). The request carries no personal data;
  like any web request, GitHub sees your IP address.
- It does not use location, contacts, the microphone, the camera, or any account.
- It contains no ads, analytics, or tracking.
- Your settings (language and time format) are stored only on your device and are
  removed when you uninstall the app.

**Permissions the app uses**

- *Run at startup* (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`): to keep the home screen widget up to date
  after the phone restarts.
- *Alarms & reminders* (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`): optional, to switch the widget to the
  next prayer exactly on time.

**Android Auto**

When connected to a car, the app shows the same prayer times on the car screen. No data
is sent to the car maker or to anyone else.
